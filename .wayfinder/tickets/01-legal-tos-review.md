# Ticket: Legal verdict — ToS review + Markedsføringsloven for 6 chains

**Labels**: `wayfinder:research`

## Question

For each of the 6 target chains (Netto, Rema 1000, Lidl, Bilka, Føtex, SuperBrugsen):
1. What does their website ToS/robots.txt say about scraping?
2. What does Danish Markedsføringsloven (Marketing Practices Act) say about scraping publicly available prices for personal/commercial use?
3. What is the community precedent (open source projects, prior art, cease-and-desist history)?
4. Risk assessment: green/yellow/red per chain, with rationale.

**Deliverable**: A research report (Markdown) with findings per chain, risk matrix, and go/no-go recommendation for each. Published on a `research/legal-tos-review` branch with context pointer from this ticket.
